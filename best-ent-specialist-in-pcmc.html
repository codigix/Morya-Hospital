When Should You Visit an ENT Specialist?

Consult an ENT specialist if you experience:

  • ✔ Persistent ear pain
  • ✔ Hearing loss
  • ✔ Ringing in the ears
  • ✔ Chronic sinus infections
  • ✔ Nasal blockage
  • ✔ Frequent sore throat
  • ✔ Difficulty swallowing
  • ✔ Snoring or sleep-related breathing problems
  • ✔ Vertigo
  • ✔ Hoarseness lasting more than two weeks

What is an ECG Test?

An Electrocardiogram (ECG) is a simple, painless, and non-invasive test that records the electrical activity of the heart. It helps doctors detect abnormal heart rhythms, heart attacks, enlarged heart chambers, and other cardiovascular conditions.

Our Spine Care Services

01

Resting ECG

A resting ECG records your heart's electrical activity while you are relaxed.

It helps diagnose:

  • Heart rhythm disorders
  • Chest pain
  • Palpitations
  • Heart attack
  • Heart disease

02

Emergency ECG

Patients with symptoms suggestive of a heart emergency receive immediate ECG evaluation.

Emergency Symptoms Include:

  • Chest pain
  • Shortness of breath
  • Dizziness
  • Fainting
  • Irregular heartbeat

03

Cardiac Evaluation

ECG testing is often combined with a comprehensive heart assessment.

Services Include:

  • Cardiologist consultation
  • Blood pressure monitoring
  • Heart rate assessment
  • Cardiac risk evaluation
  • Treatment planning

04

ECG for Health Check-ups

ECG forms an important part of preventive health screening for individuals with cardiovascular risk factors.

Recommended For:

  • High blood pressure
  • Diabetes
  • High cholesterol
  • Family history of heart disease
  • Adults above 40 years (as advised by a doctor)

05

Conditions Detected with ECG

An ECG may help identify:

  • Irregular heartbeat (Arrhythmia)
  • Heart attack
  • Coronary artery disease
  • Heart enlargement
  • Conduction abnormalities
  • Electrolyte-related cardiac changes
  • Certain structural heart conditions

Symptoms That Need an ECG Test

An ECG may help identify:

  • Chest pain
  • Palpitations
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Dizziness
  • Fainting
  • Shortness of breath
  • Unexplained fatigue
  • High blood pressure

Early diagnosis can help prevent serious cardiac complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What is an ECG test used for?

An ECG records the electrical activity of the heart and helps diagnose heart rhythm disorders, heart attacks, and other cardiac conditions.

Is an ECG painful?

No. An ECG is a safe, painless, and non-invasive procedure that usually takes only a few minutes.

When should I get an ECG?

Your doctor may recommend an ECG if you have chest pain, palpitations, dizziness, shortness of breath, or a history of heart disease.

How long does an ECG test take?

A standard resting ECG typically takes between 5 and 10 minutes.

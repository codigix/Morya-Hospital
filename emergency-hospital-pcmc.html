Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Emergency Hospital in PCMC, providing 24x7 emergency medical services for accidents, trauma, cardiac emergencies, stroke, respiratory distress, fractures, burns, poisoning, and other life-threatening conditions. Our emergency department is equipped with modern medical technology and supported by experienced emergency physicians, intensivists, surgeons, orthopaedic specialists, cardiologists, neurologists, and critical care nurses to deliver immediate medical attention when every second counts. The hospital offers emergency care, ICU support, trauma services, and specialist departments under one roof.
We serve patients across PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Akurdi, Ravet, Wakad, Bhosari, and Pune with rapid diagnosis, emergency treatment, ICU care, surgical support, and comprehensive critical care services.

Symptoms That Need Emergency Medical Attention

Visit the emergency department immediately if you experience:

  • Severe chest pain
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Stroke symptoms
  • Major accidents or trauma
  • Severe bleeding
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Head injury
  • High fever with altered mental status
  • Seizures
  • Severe allergic reactions

Early diagnosis can improve treatment outcomes and help avoid further spinal damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

When should I visit the emergency department?

Visit immediately if you have severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, major injuries, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, or any life-threatening condition.

Does Moraya Hospital provide 24×7 emergency services?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers round-the-clock emergency medical care, ICU support, trauma management, and specialist consultations.

Is an ICU available for critically ill patients?

Yes. Our hospital has an Intensive Care Unit equipped with advanced monitoring systems, ventilator support, and experienced critical care specialists.

Are emergency diagnostic services available?

Yes. We provide emergency CT Scan, Digital X-ray, ECG, laboratory investigations, and other diagnostic services to support rapid diagnosis and treatment.

Does Moraya Hospital treat accident and trauma cases?

Yes. Our emergency department provides immediate assessment and treatment for road traffic accidents, fractures, head injuries, trauma, and other emergency conditions.

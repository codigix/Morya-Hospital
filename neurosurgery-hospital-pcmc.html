Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Neurosurgery Hospital in PCMC offering advanced diagnosis, surgical treatment, and rehabilitation for brain, spine, and nervous system disorders. Our experienced neurosurgeons use modern surgical techniques and advanced technology to provide personalised care for both emergency and planned neurological conditions.
We serve patients across PCMC, Chinchwad, and Pune with comprehensive neurosurgical care focused on accurate diagnosis, safe treatment, and faster recovery.

Our Neurosurgery Services

01

Brain Surgery & Neurological Disorders

We provide expert surgical care for various brain-related conditions affecting neurological function and quality of life.

Conditions Treated:

  • Brain tumours
  • Head injuries
  • Stroke-related complications
  • Brain haemorrhage
  • Hydrocephalus
  • Brain infections requiring surgical management

02

Spine Surgery & Spinal Disorders:

Our spine specialists treat conditions affecting the spine, spinal cord, and surrounding nerves using advanced surgical and non-surgical approaches.

Conditions Include:

  • Slip disc (herniated disc)
  • Spinal stenosis
  • Chronic back pain
  • Neck pain related to spinal disorders
  • Spinal cord compression
  • Spinal fractures and injuries

03

Neuro Trauma & Emergency Neurosurgery

We provide emergency neurosurgical care for patients with serious head, brain, or spinal injuries requiring immediate medical attention.

Emergency Conditions Treated:

  • Traumatic brain injury
  • Road accident-related head injuries
  • Spinal trauma
  • Brain bleeding
  • Severe neurological emergencies

04

Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery

Our neurosurgeons use minimally invasive techniques whenever suitable to reduce pain, minimise hospital stay, and support faster recovery.

Benefits Include:

  • Smaller incisions
  • Reduced blood loss
  • Less postoperative pain
  • Faster recovery and rehabilitation

05

Post-Surgery Rehabilitation & Follow-Up

Recovery after neurosurgery is supported through structured rehabilitation and long-term follow-up care.

Services Include:

  • Physiotherapy support
  • Mobility rehabilitation
  • Pain management
  • Neurological monitoring
  • Postoperative counselling and follow-up

Symptoms That Need Neurosurgery Consultation

Consult a neurosurgeon if you experience:

  • Persistent severe headache
  • Shortness of breath
  • Sudden weakness or paralysis
  • Difficulty speaking or swallowing
  • Severe neck or back pain
  • Numbness or tingling in limbs
  • Seizures
  • Head injury or unconsciousness
  • Loss of bladder or bowel control

Early diagnosis and treatment can help prevent serious neurological complications and improve recovery outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What conditions are treated by a neurosurgeon?

Neurosurgeons treat disorders affecting the brain, spine, spinal cord, and nerves, including brain tumours, slip disc, spinal stenosis, head injuries, and neurological emergencies.

Is spine surgery always necessary for slip disc?

No, many slip disc cases improve with medication, physiotherapy, and non-surgical treatment. Surgery is considered when symptoms are severe or do not improve with conservative care.

Do you provide emergency neurosurgery care?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides emergency neurosurgical evaluation and treatment for head injuries, brain bleeding, spinal trauma, and other neurological emergencies.

How long does recovery after neurosurgery take?

Recovery time varies depending on the condition and procedure performed. Some patients recover within weeks, while others may require longer rehabilitation and follow-up care.

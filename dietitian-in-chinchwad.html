Introduction

Good nutrition is one of the most important foundations for a healthy life. At Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital, Chinchwad, we provide comprehensive dietitian and clinical nutrition services to help patients manage medical conditions, achieve healthy weight goals, and improve overall well-being through evidence-based nutrition.
Led by Dt. Shalini Garg, an experienced clinical dietitian with over 15 years of expertise, our nutrition department offers personalised diet counselling for individuals of all age groups—from infants and children to adults, pregnant women, and senior citizens.
Our approach focuses on practical, sustainable, and scientifically designed meal plans that fit your lifestyle while supporting long-term health.

Comprehensive Dietitian Services

01

Personalized Diet Planning

Every individual has different nutritional requirements. Our customised diet plans are created after evaluating your medical history, lifestyle, food preferences, and health goals.

Our Diet Plans Include:

  • Personalized meal planning
  • Weight loss diet plans
  • Healthy weight gain programs

  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian meal plans

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What conditions can a clinical dietitian help manage?

Our clinical dietitian helps manage diabetes, hypertension, obesity, PCOS, thyroid disorders, kidney disease, liver disease, heart disease, digestive disorders, and many other medical conditions through personalized nutrition therapy.

Do you provide personalized diet plans?

Yes. Every diet plan is customized according to your age, weight, medical condition, lifestyle, nutritional needs, and food preferences.

Can a dietitian help with weight loss and weight gain?

Absolutely. We offer scientifically designed weight loss and healthy weight gain programs that focus on sustainable, long-term results without crash dieting.

Do you provide nutrition counselling during pregnancy?

Yes. We provide trimester-wise pregnancy diet plans, gestational diabetes management, anaemia nutrition, lactation counselling, and postnatal recovery diets.

Is pediatric nutrition available?

Yes. We offer specialised nutrition counselling for infants, toddlers, children, and adolescents to support healthy growth, immunity, and development.

How often should I consult a dietitian?

The frequency depends on your health condition and goals. Most patients benefit from regular follow-up consultations every 2–4 weeks to monitor progress and adjust their diet plans.

Who can benefit from consulting a dietitian?

YAnyone looking to improve their health can benefit, including individuals with chronic diseases, children, pregnant women, athletes, senior citizens, and those seeking weight management or preventive nutrition guidance.

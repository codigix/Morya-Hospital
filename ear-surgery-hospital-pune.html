Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Ear Surgery Hospital in Pune, offering advanced surgical treatment for a wide range of ear disorders affecting hearing, balance, and ear health. Our experienced ENT specialists provide comprehensive diagnosis, modern surgical care, and personalized treatment plans for both adults and children.
We serve patients across Pune, PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, and surrounding areas with expert ear care, hearing restoration procedures, and advanced ENT surgical services.

Advanced Ear Surgery Services

Our ENT department specializes in diagnosing and treating complex ear conditions that may require surgical intervention to improve hearing, eliminate infections, and restore ear function.

01

Ear Infection Surgery

Chronic or recurrent ear infections can damage the ear and affect hearing if left untreated.

Conditions Treated:

  • Chronic ear infections
  • Recurrent ear discharge
  • Middle ear infections
  • Ear drum perforation
  • Persistent ear pain

02

Eardrum Repair Surgery (Tympanoplasty)

A perforated eardrum can lead to hearing loss, infections, and discomfort.

Benefits of Tympanoplasty:

  • Repair of damaged eardrum
  • Improved hearing ability
  • Reduced risk of infections
  • Enhanced ear health

03

Mastoid Surgery (Mastoidectomy)

Mastoid surgery may be recommended when infections spread to the mastoid bone behind the ear.

Conditions Treated:

  • Chronic ear disease
  • Mastoid infections
  • Cholesteatoma
  • Recurrent ear infections

04

Services Include:

Certain ear conditions can lead to conductive hearing loss that may improve with surgical treatment.

Services Include:

  • Hearing evaluation
  • Surgical consultation
  • Hearing improvement procedures
  • Post-operative hearing assessment

05

Ear Tube Surgery (Grommet Insertion)

Ear tube placement may help children and adults suffering from recurrent ear infections or fluid accumulation in the middle ear.

Benefits Include:

  • Improved hearing
  • Reduced infections
  • Better ear ventilation
  • Enhanced speech development in children

06

Cholesteatoma Treatment

Cholesteatoma is an abnormal skin growth in the middle ear that can damage hearing and surrounding structures if untreated.

Symptoms Include:

  • Ear discharge
  • Hearing loss
  • Ear fullness
  • Dizziness

Conditions That May Require Ear Surgery

Consult an ENT specialist if you experience:

  • Chronic ear infections
  • Persistent ear discharge
  • Hearing loss
  • Perforated eardrum
  • Ear pain
  • Recurrent middle ear infections
  • Cholesteatoma
  • Balance problems
  • Ear-related dizziness

Early diagnosis and treatment can help prevent long-term hearing complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

When is ear surgery required?

Ear surgery may be recommended for chronic infections, perforated eardrums, cholesteatoma, hearing loss, or recurrent ear problems that do not respond to medical treatment.

Can ear surgery improve hearing?

Yes. Certain ear surgeries can help restore or improve hearing depending on the underlying condition.

Is ear surgery safe?

Ear surgeries are commonly performed by experienced ENT surgeons and are generally safe when conducted after proper evaluation.

Do children require ear surgery?

Some children with recurrent ear infections, fluid accumulation, or hearing problems may benefit from ear procedures such as grommet insertion.

Does Moraya Hospital provide hearing tests before ear surgery?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides audiometry testing, hearing evaluations, and comprehensive ENT assessments before recommending surgery.

Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Knee Replacement Hospital in Pune, offering comprehensive diagnosis, advanced knee replacement surgery, and rehabilitation for patients suffering from severe knee pain and joint damage. Our experienced orthopaedic surgeons provide personalized treatment plans to help patients regain mobility, reduce pain, and return to an active lifestyle.
We serve patients across Pune, PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and nearby areas with modern orthopaedic care, advanced surgical techniques, physiotherapy, and post-operative rehabilitation. Comprehensive care before, during, and after surgery is an important part of successful knee replacement recovery.

What is Knee Replacement Surgery?

Knee replacement surgery (Knee Arthroplasty) is a procedure in which damaged portions of the knee joint are replaced with artificial implants. It is usually recommended for patients with severe arthritis or knee damage when medications, injections, and physiotherapy no longer provide adequate relief.

Symptoms That May Need Knee Replacement

Consult an orthopaedic specialist if you experience:

  • Severe knee pain
  • Difficulty walking
  • Knee stiffness
  • Swollen knee joints
  • Pain while climbing stairs
  • Knee deformity
  • Reduced joint movement
  • Difficulty standing for long periods
  • Persistent arthritis pain

Early evaluation helps determine the most appropriate treatment and whether surgery is necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

Who needs knee replacement surgery?

Knee replacement may be recommended for patients with severe arthritis, chronic knee pain, joint damage, or significant mobility limitations that do not improve with conservative treatment.

How long does recovery take after knee replacement?

Recovery varies from person to person. Most patients begin walking with assistance soon after surgery and continue rehabilitation over the following weeks and months.

Is physiotherapy necessary after knee replacement?

Yes. Physiotherapy is an important part of recovery and helps improve strength, flexibility, mobility, and overall function.

Can both knees be replaced?

Depending on your overall health and medical evaluation, your orthopaedic surgeon will determine whether one or both knees should be replaced.

Does Moraya Hospital provide rehabilitation after knee replacement?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers physiotherapy, rehabilitation, follow-up care, and comprehensive recovery support after knee replacement surgery.

Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers reliable and accurate Laboratory & Blood Investigation Services in PCMC with advanced diagnostic facilities and experienced pathology professionals. Our laboratory supports early disease detection, health monitoring, preventive screenings, and accurate diagnosis through a wide range of blood tests and laboratory investigations.
We provide comprehensive diagnostic services for patients across PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and Pune with a commitment to accuracy, timely reporting, and quality healthcare.

Our Laboratory Services

01

Complete Blood Count (CBC)

A CBC test helps evaluate overall health and detect a variety of conditions including infections, anaemia, and blood disorders.

Benefits Include:

  • Infection detection
  • Anaemia evaluation
  • General health assessment
  • Monitoring ongoing treatments

02

Diabetes & Blood Sugar Testing

Regular blood sugar testing is essential for diagnosing and managing diabetes.

Services Include:

  • Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)
  • Postprandial Blood Sugar (PPBS)
  • HbA1c Test
  • Diabetes Monitoring Tests

03

Thyroid Function Tests

Thyroid tests help assess hormone levels and identify thyroid-related disorders.

Conditions Evaluated:

  • Hypothyroidism
  • Hyperthyroidism
  • Thyroid hormone imbalance
  • Metabolic disorders

04

Liver Function Tests (LFT)

Liver investigations help monitor liver health and detect liver diseases.

Tests Include:

  • Bilirubin
  • SGOT
  • SGPT
  • Liver enzyme evaluation

05

Kidney Function Tests (KFT)

Kidney function tests assess how effectively the kidneys filter waste from the body.

Services Include:

  • Serum Creatinine
  • Blood Urea
  • Electrolyte Analysis
  • Kidney Health Assessment

06

Lipid Profile Testing

A lipid profile helps evaluate cardiovascular risk and cholesterol levels.

Parameters Include:

  • Total Cholesterol
  • HDL Cholesterol
  • LDL Cholesterol
  • Triglycerides

07

Infection & Fever Profile Testing

We provide diagnostic testing for various infectious diseases and fever-related conditions.

Investigations Include:

  • Dengue Test
  • Malaria Test
  • Typhoid Test
  • Infection Screening
  • Fever Evaluation Panels

Preventive Health Checkup Packages

Regular health screenings can help detect diseases at an early stage.
Health Checkups Include:

  • Complete Blood Tests
  • Diabetes Screening
  • Liver & Kidney Function Tests
  • Thyroid Profile
  • Cholesterol Screening
  • General Health Assessment

Why Are Blood Investigations Important?

Laboratory investigations help doctors:

  • Diagnose diseases accurately
  • Monitor ongoing treatments
  • Detect health problems early
  • Assess organ function
  • Prevent complications
  • Support preventive healthcare

Timely blood investigations can play a critical role in maintaining overall health and well-being.

Symptoms That May Require Blood Tests

Consult your doctor if you experience:

  • Persistent fever
  • Unexplained fatigue
  • Sudden weight changes
  • Weakness or dizziness
  • Frequent infections
  • High blood sugar symptoms
  • High blood pressure
  • Thyroid-related symptoms
  • General health concerns

Early trauma intervention can significantly improve recovery and reduce complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What blood tests are available at Moraya Hospital?

We offer CBC, blood sugar tests, thyroid profile, liver function tests, kidney function tests, lipid profile, infection screening, and preventive health checkup packages.

How long does it take to receive laboratory reports?

Report availability depends on the type of test. Many routine investigations are processed and reported promptly.

Do I need fasting before a blood test?

Certain tests such as fasting blood sugar and lipid profile may require fasting. Our team will guide you before sample collection.

Why are regular blood tests important?

Regular blood investigations help detect diseases early, monitor health conditions, and support preventive healthcare.

Do you provide preventive health checkup packages?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers comprehensive preventive health checkup packages for individuals and families.

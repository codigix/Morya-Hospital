Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Vaccination Centre in PCMC, offering comprehensive immunization services for infants, children, adolescents, adults, and senior citizens. Vaccination plays a vital role in preventing infectious diseases and protecting long-term health.
Our experienced healthcare professionals follow recommended vaccination schedules and provide safe, effective, and timely immunization services. We serve families across PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and Pune with patient-focused vaccination and preventive healthcare services.

Importance of Vaccination

Vaccines help the body's immune system recognize and fight harmful infections before they cause serious illness.
Benefits of Vaccination Include:

  • Protection against infectious diseases
  • Stronger immune system
  • Reduced risk of complications
  • Prevention of disease outbreaks
  • Long-term health protection
  • Community immunity

Early diagnosis and treatment help prevent complications and promote healthy development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

Why is vaccination important?

Vaccination helps protect against infectious diseases and reduces the risk of severe illness and complications.

Are vaccines safe?

Yes. Vaccines undergo extensive testing and monitoring to ensure safety and effectiveness.

When should children start receiving vaccines?

Many vaccinations begin shortly after birth and continue according to recommended childhood immunization schedules.

Do adults need vaccinations?

Yes. Adults may require booster doses and vaccines for influenza, hepatitis, tetanus, travel-related diseases, and other preventable infections.

Does Moraya Hospital provide vaccination services for all age groups?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides vaccination and immunization services for newborns, children, adolescents, adults, and senior citizens.

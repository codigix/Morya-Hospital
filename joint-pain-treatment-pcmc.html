What is Joint Pain?

Joint pain refers to discomfort, stiffness, swelling, or reduced movement in one or more joints. It can affect daily activities such as walking, climbing stairs, exercising, or performing routine tasks.
Joint pain may result from injury, arthritis, age-related degeneration, inflammation, or underlying medical conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What causes joint pain?

Joint pain may be caused by arthritis, injuries, inflammation, overuse, ageing, ligament damage, or underlying medical conditions.

When should I consult a doctor for joint pain?

You should consult a specialist if your joint pain persists, worsens, causes swelling, limits movement, or affects daily activities.

Can physiotherapy help joint pain?

Yes. Physiotherapy can improve flexibility, strengthen muscles, reduce pain, and support joint recovery.

Is arthritis a common cause of joint pain?

Yes. Osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis are among the most common causes of chronic joint pain.

Does Moraya Hospital provide rehabilitation for joint disorders?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers physiotherapy, rehabilitation, arthritis management, and comprehensive orthopaedic care.

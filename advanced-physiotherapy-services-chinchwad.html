Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers comprehensive physiotherapy and rehabilitation services for pain relief, mobility improvement, injury recovery, and physical rehabilitation. Our experienced physiotherapists provide personalised therapy programs using modern rehabilitation techniques and patient-focused care.
We help patients across Chinchwad, PCMC, and Pune recover from injuries, surgeries, neurological conditions, and musculoskeletal disorders with effective physiotherapy treatment.

Our Physiotherapy Services

01

Back Pain Physiotherapy

We provide specialised physiotherapy treatment for chronic and acute back pain conditions to improve movement and reduce discomfort.

Conditions Treated:

  • Lower back pain
  • Upper back pain
  • Slip disc-related pain
  • Muscle stiffness
  • Postural back pain

02

Joint Pain & Arthritis Physiotherapy

Our physiotherapists help improve joint movement and reduce pain caused by arthritis and musculoskeletal disorders.

Conditions included:

  • Knee pain
  • Shoulder pain
  • Arthritis-related stiffness
  • Joint mobility issues
  • Frozen shoulder

03

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

We provide rehabilitation programs for sports injuries to help patients recover strength, flexibility, and mobility.

Sports Injuries Treated::

  • Ligament injuries
  • Muscle strain
  • Sports-related joint injuries
  • Sprains and stiffness
  • Post-injury rehabilitation

04

Post-Surgery Rehabilitation

Our physiotherapy team supports recovery after orthopaedic and neurological surgeries to restore mobility and strength.

Rehabilitation Includes:

  • Knee replacement rehabilitation
  • Fracture recovery physiotherapy
  • Spine surgery rehabilitation
  • Joint surgery recovery
  • Mobility improvement therapy

05

Neurological Physiotherapy

We provide physiotherapy care for neurological conditions affecting movement, balance, and coordination.

Conditions Treated:

  • Paralysis rehabilitation
  • Stroke recovery
  • Nerve weakness
  • Balance disorders
  • Neurological mobility problems

06

Pain Management Therapy

Our therapy programs focus on reducing pain, improving flexibility, and restoring physical function for better quality of life.

Benefits of Physiotherapy

  • Pain relief without surgery
  • Improved mobility and flexibility
  • Faster recovery after injury or surgery
  • Better posture and balance
  • Improved muscle strength
  • Long-term rehabilitation support

Symptoms That Need Physiotherapy Consultation

Consult a physiotherapist if you experience:

  • Chronic back or neck pain
  • Joint stiffness
  • Difficulty walking or moving
  • Sports injuries
  • Muscle weakness
  • Post-surgery mobility issues
  • Nerve pain or paralysis
  • Reduced flexibility

Early physiotherapy can improve recovery and prevent complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What conditions are treated with physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy helps treat back pain, joint pain, sports injuries, paralysis, arthritis, muscle weakness, and post-surgery recovery conditions.

Do you provide physiotherapy for paralysis recovery?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides neurological physiotherapy and paralysis rehabilitation services.

Is physiotherapy helpful after surgery?

Yes, physiotherapy improves mobility, strength, flexibility, and recovery after surgery.

How many physiotherapy sessions are needed?

The number of sessions depends on the condition, severity, and recovery progress of the patient

Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Stroke Treatment Hospital in PCMC, providing rapid diagnosis, emergency stroke management, neurological care, ICU support, and rehabilitation services. Our experienced neurologists, neurosurgeons, intensivists, and rehabilitation specialists work together to deliver timely treatment for patients with ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke. Early recognition and treatment—especially within the first few hours—can significantly improve recovery and reduce long-term disability.
We serve patients across PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and Pune with comprehensive stroke care, advanced brain imaging, intensive care, and post-stroke rehabilitation.

What is a Stroke?

A stroke occurs when the blood supply to the brain is interrupted due to a blocked artery (ischemic stroke) or a ruptured blood vessel (hemorrhagic stroke). Stroke is a medical emergency that requires immediate evaluation and treatment to minimize brain damage.

Stroke Symptoms That Need Immediate Medical Attention

Seek emergency medical care if you experience:

  • Sudden weakness of the face, arm, or leg
  • Facial drooping
  • Difficulty speaking or understanding speech
  • Sudden vision loss
  • Severe headache
  • Loss of balance or coordination
  • Sudden dizziness
  • Confusion or unconsciousness

Do not delay treatment. Every minute counts during a stroke emergency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What are the early signs of a stroke?

Common signs include sudden weakness, facial drooping, speech difficulty, vision problems, severe headache, dizziness, and loss of balance.

Is stroke a medical emergency?

Yes. Stroke requires immediate medical attention. Early diagnosis and treatment can significantly improve recovery and reduce permanent disability.

What tests are performed to diagnose a stroke?

Doctors may recommend CT Scan, MRI, blood investigations, neurological examination, and cardiac evaluation.

Does Moraya Hospital provide rehabilitation after stroke?

Yes. We provide physiotherapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy, and long-term rehabilitation to help patients regain mobility and independence.

Does Moraya Hospital provide emergency stroke care?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides emergency stroke evaluation, ICU care, neurological treatment, diagnostic imaging, and rehabilitation services.

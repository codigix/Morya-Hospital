Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers advanced CT Scan services in PCMC, providing fast, accurate, and reliable diagnostic imaging to support early disease detection and effective treatment planning. Our CT Scan Centre is equipped with modern imaging technology and is supported by experienced radiologists and trained technicians, ensuring high-quality diagnostic services with prompt reporting.
We serve patients across PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and Pune with comprehensive CT imaging for neurological, chest, abdominal, orthopaedic, trauma, and vascular conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

Why is a CT Scan performed?

A CT Scan helps diagnose injuries, infections, tumours, internal bleeding, fractures, stroke, and various diseases affecting different parts of the body.

Is a CT Scan safe?

CT Scans are generally safe when performed under medical supervision. Your doctor will recommend the scan only when it is clinically necessary.

How long does a CT Scan take?

Most CT Scan procedures are completed within 10 to 30 minutes, depending on the type of examination.

Do I need to prepare before a CT Scan?

Some CT scans may require fasting or contrast dye. Your healthcare provider will give you specific preparation instructions before the procedure.

Does Moraya Hospital provide CT Scan services in PCMC?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides advanced CT Scan services with expert radiology support and timely diagnostic reporting.

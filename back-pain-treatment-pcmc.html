What is Back Pain?

Back pain is one of the most common musculoskeletal conditions affecting people of all ages. It may occur due to injury, poor posture, muscle strain, spinal disorders, or age-related degeneration.
Back pain can affect daily activities, work performance, sleep quality, and overall quality of life if left untreated.

Common Causes of Back Pain

Our specialists diagnose and manage various causes of back pain, including:

  • Blurred or cloudy vision
  • Difficulty seeing at night
  • Increased sensitivity to light
  • Glare from headlights
  • Frequent changes in eyeglass prescription
  • Faded or yellowed colours
  • Double vision in one eye
  • Difficulty reading or driving

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What causes chronic back pain?

Chronic back pain may result from muscle strain, poor posture, slip disc, arthritis, spine degeneration, injuries, or nerve-related conditions.

When should I see a doctor for back pain?

You should consult a specialist if your back pain persists for several weeks, affects daily activities, causes numbness, or radiates to your legs.

Can physiotherapy help back pain?

Yes. Physiotherapy can strengthen muscles, improve flexibility, correct posture, and reduce pain for many back conditions.

What is sciatica?

Sciatica is nerve pain caused by irritation or compression of the sciatic nerve, often resulting in pain radiating from the lower back to the leg.

Does Moraya Hospital provide physiotherapy for back pain?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers physiotherapy, rehabilitation, orthopaedic consultation, and comprehensive spine care for back pain patients.

Is surgery always required for back pain?

No. Many back pain conditions can be effectively managed with medication, physiotherapy, lifestyle changes, and non-surgical treatments. Surgery is considered only when necessary based on the patient's condition.

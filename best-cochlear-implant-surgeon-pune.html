What is a Cochlear Implant?

A cochlear implant is a sophisticated electronic hearing device designed for individuals with severe to profound sensorineural hearing loss who gain little or no benefit from conventional hearing aids. Unlike hearing aids that simply amplify sound, cochlear implants directly stimulate the auditory nerve, helping patients hear sounds more clearly and improve communication abilities.

Who Needs Cochlear Implant Surgery?

You may be a candidate for cochlear implant treatment if you experience:

  • Severe hearing loss
  • Profound hearing loss
  • Difficulty understanding speech
  • Limited benefit from hearing aids
  • Congenital hearing impairment
  • Progressive hearing loss
  • Delayed speech development in children
  • Hearing difficulties affecting education or daily life

A detailed hearing evaluation is necessary to determine cochlear implant eligibility.

Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Trauma Care Hospital in Chinchwad, PCMC, providing immediate medical attention and emergency treatment for accident victims, injuries, fractures, head trauma, and other life-threatening conditions. Our dedicated emergency and trauma care team is available to deliver rapid assessment, stabilisation, critical care, and surgical intervention when every second matters.
We provide comprehensive trauma and emergency care services for patients across Chinchwad, PCMC, Pune, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and surrounding areas. Modern trauma care centres typically rely on rapid triage, emergency stabilisation, advanced imaging, multidisciplinary specialists, and critical care support to improve patient outcomes.

Conditions That Require Immediate Trauma Care

Seek emergency medical attention if you experience:

  • Road accident injuries
  • Severe bleeding
  • Head injury
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Fractures or dislocations
  • Chest injuries
  • Severe burns
  • Spinal injuries
  • Deep cuts or wounds
  • Difficulty breathing after an accident

Early trauma intervention can significantly improve recovery and reduce complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What is trauma care?

Trauma care involves the emergency assessment, stabilisation, treatment, and rehabilitation of patients who have suffered serious injuries due to accidents, falls, burns, or other emergencies.

When should I visit a trauma care hospital?

You should seek immediate medical attention for head injuries, fractures, severe bleeding, burns, chest injuries, spinal trauma, or any life-threatening emergency.

Does Moraya Hospital provide emergency trauma services?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides emergency trauma evaluation, critical care support, fracture management, and emergency medical treatment.

Can trauma injuries cause long-term complications?

Yes. Delayed treatment may increase the risk of complications, disability, and prolonged recovery. Early medical intervention is essential.

Do you provide ICU support for trauma patients?

Yes, trauma patients requiring intensive monitoring and advanced medical care can receive ICU and critical care support as required.

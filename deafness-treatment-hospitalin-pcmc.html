Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Deafness Treatment Hospital in Pune and PCMC, providing comprehensive diagnosis, evaluation, and treatment for hearing loss and hearing-related disorders. Our experienced ENT specialists and audiology team use advanced diagnostic technology to identify the cause of hearing impairment and recommend personalised treatment plans for children and adults.
We are committed to helping patients improve hearing, communication, and quality of life through expert hearing care, hearing rehabilitation, and advanced treatment options.

Our Deafness & Hearing Loss Treatment Services

01

Comprehensive Hearing Assessment

Early diagnosis is essential for effective treatment of hearing loss and deafness.

Services Include:

  • Hearing evaluation
  • Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA)
  • Speech audiometry
  • Hearing screening
  • Audiology consultation
  • ENT examination

02

Hearing Loss Diagnosis & Treatment

Our specialists diagnose and manage different types of hearing loss affecting children, adults, and senior citizens.

Conditions Treated:

  • Sensorineural hearing loss
  • Conductive hearing loss
  • Mixed hearing loss
  • Sudden hearing loss
  • Age-related hearing loss
  • Noise-induced hearing loss

03

Deafness Treatment for Children

Hearing problems in children can affect speech, language development, and learning abilities.

Paediatric Hearing Services Include:

  • Childhood hearing assessment
  • Newborn hearing evaluation
  • Speech and hearing development assessment
  • Parent counselling
  • Hearing rehabilitation planning
  • Cochlear implant evaluation

04

Adult Hearing Loss Treatment

Hearing impairment in adults can significantly impact communication and daily activities.

Conditions Evaluated:

  • Progressive hearing loss
  • Occupational hearing loss
  • Hearing difficulties in noisy environments
  • Communication-related hearing disorders
  • Severe hearing impairment

05

Cochlear Implant Evaluation

Patients with severe to profound hearing loss who receive limited benefit from hearing aids may be candidates for cochlear implant treatment.

Services Include:

  • Cochlear implant candidacy assessment
  • Advanced hearing evaluation
  • ENT consultation
  • Pre-operative counselling
  • Hearing rehabilitation planning

06

Hearing Rehabilitation Services

Our audiology team provides comprehensive rehabilitation support to help patients improve communication and hearing outcomes.

Services Include:

  • Audiology guidance
  • Hearing rehabilitation programs
  • Listening skill development
  • Speech therapy support
  • Follow-up hearing assessments
  • Long-term hearing care

Symptoms That Need Hearing Evaluation

Consult our ENT specialist if you experience:

  • Difficulty hearing conversations
  • Frequently asking people to repeat themselves
  • Ringing in the ears (tinnitus)
  • Muffled hearing
  • Sudden hearing loss
  • Difficulty understanding speech
  • Delayed speech development in children
  • Hearing difficulties affecting work or education

Early diagnosis and treatment can help prevent further hearing deterioration and improve communication abilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What causes deafness and hearing loss?

Hearing loss may occur due to aging, infections, excessive noise exposure, genetic conditions, ear disorders, injury, or nerve-related problems.

Can deafness be treated?

Treatment depends on the cause and severity of hearing loss. Many hearing conditions can be managed with medical treatment, hearing rehabilitation, or advanced hearing solutions such as cochlear implants.

When should I consult a hearing specialist?

You should seek medical evaluation if you notice hearing difficulties, tinnitus, sudden hearing loss, delayed speech development in children, or communication problems.

Do you provide hearing tests at Moraya Hospital?

Yes. Moraya Hospital offers comprehensive hearing assessments, audiometry testing, and audiology services for children and adults.

Who may need a cochlear implant?

Patients with severe to profound hearing loss who receive limited benefit from hearing aids may be evaluated for cochlear implant treatment.

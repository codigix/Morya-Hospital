What Is a Cochlear Implant?

A cochlear implant is an advanced electronic hearing device designed for people with severe to profound sensorineural hearing loss who receive limited benefit from hearing aids. Unlike hearing aids that amplify sounds, a cochlear implant bypasses damaged hair cells in the inner ear and directly stimulates the auditory nerve, allowing the brain to interpret sound more effectively.
A cochlear implant system includes:

  • ✔ External speech processor
  • ✔ Internal implant
  • ✔ Electrode array
  • ✔ Sound transmission system

Following surgery, patients undergo activation and auditory rehabilitation to maximise hearing and speech outcomes.

Signs You May Need a Cochlear Implant

You should consult an experienced cochlear implant surgeon if you or your child experiences:

  • ✔ Severe or profound hearing loss
  • ✔ Difficulty understanding speech despite hearing aids
  • ✔ Delayed speech and language development in children
  • ✔ Frequent requests for repetition
  • ✔ Difficulty hearing conversations in noisy environments
  • ✔ Hearing loss affecting school, work, or daily communication

Early diagnosis is especially important for children, as timely intervention supports speech and language development.

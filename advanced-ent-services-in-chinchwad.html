Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers comprehensive ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat) services for patients experiencing ear, nose, throat, hearing, and sinus-related conditions. Our experienced ENT specialists provide advanced diagnosis and personalised treatment using modern medical technology and patient-focused care.
We serve patients across Chinchwad, PCMC, and Pune with expert ENT care focused on accurate diagnosis, effective treatment, and faster recovery.

Our ENT Services

01

Ear Disorder Treatment

We diagnose and treat various ear-related conditions affecting hearing, balance, and ear health.

Ear Conditions Treated:

  • Ear Infection
  • Hearing loss
  • Ear pain
  • Ear blockage
  • Tinnitus (ringing in ears)
  • Vertigo and balance disorders

02

Nose & Sinus Treatment

Our ENT specialists provide advanced treatment for nasal and sinus disorders affecting breathing and overall comfort.

Conditions Treated:

  • Sinusitis
  • Nasal allergy
  • Nasal blockage
  • Deviated nasal septum (DNS)
  • Chronic cold and sneezing
  • Nose bleeding

03

Throat & Tonsil Treatment

We offer comprehensive care for throat infections, tonsil problems, voice disorders, and swallowing difficulties.

Throat Conditions Include:

  • Tonsillitis
  • Sore throat
  • Voice changes
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Throat infection
  • Chronic cough

04

Hearing Evaluation & Audiology Support

Our hospital provides hearing assessments and audiology support for patients with hearing and balance-related problems.

Services Include:

  • Hearing tests
  • Audiometry
  • Hearing loss evaluation
  • Balance assessment

05

Vertigo & Balance Disorder Treatment

We diagnose and manage dizziness, vertigo, and inner ear balance disorders affecting daily activities.

06

ENT Surgical Procedures

Our ENT department provides advanced surgical care for selected ear, nose, and throat conditions when required.

Procedures May Include:

  • Tonsil surgery
  • Ear procedures
  • Sinus-related procedures
  • Nasal corrective procedures

Symptoms That Need ENT Consultation

Consult an ENT specialist if you experience:

  • Ear pain or hearing loss
  • Frequent throat infection
  • Persistent cough
  • Nasal blockage
  • Sinus pain or headache
  • Vertigo or dizziness
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Ringing sound in ears

Early diagnosis helps prevent complications and improves recovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What does an ENT specialist treat?

An ENT specialist treats ear, nose, throat, sinus, hearing, voice, and balance-related disorders.

Do you provide hearing tests in Chinchwad?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides hearing evaluation and audiology support services.

When should I consult an ENT doctor?

You should consult an ENT specialist if you have hearing loss, sinus problems, throat infections, dizziness, or persistent nasal blockage.

Can sinus problems become serious?

Yes, untreated sinus infections may lead to chronic discomfort, breathing problems, and recurring infections.

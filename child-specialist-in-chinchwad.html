Who is a Child Specialist?

A child specialist, also known as a pediatrician, is a doctor who specializes in diagnosing, treating, and preventing diseases and health conditions affecting infants, children, and teenagers.
Pediatricians focus on:

  • Newborn care
  • Child growth and development
  • Vaccinations and immunization
  • Nutritional guidance
  • Treatment of childhood illnesses
  • Preventive healthcare
  • Adolescent health management

Regular visits to a child specialist help detect health issues early and support healthy physical and mental development.

Common Conditions Treated by a Child Specialist

01

Fever and Infections

Children frequently experience infections due to developing immune systems.

Common illnesses include:

  • Viral fever
  • Cold and cough
  • Flu
  • Ear infections
  • Throat infections
  • Seasonal infections

02

Respiratory Problems

Breathing difficulties require timely medical attention.

Conditions include:

  • Asthma
  • Wheezing
  • Bronchitis
  • Respiratory infections
  • Allergic cough

03

Digestive Disorders

Digestive problems are common among children and infants.

Conditions treated include:

  • Diarrhoea
  • Constipation
  • Vomiting
  • Stomach pain
  • Feeding difficulties

04

Growth and Development Concerns

Regular monitoring ensures children achieve developmental milestones appropriately.

Areas assessed include:

  • Height and weight growth
  • Speech developments
  • Physical development
  • Behavioural development
  • Nutritional status

Importance of Childhood Vaccination

Vaccination is one of the most effective ways to protect children from serious diseases.
Benefits of immunization include:

  • Protection against infections
  • Stronger immune system
  • Reduced risk of complications
  • Community health protection
  • Long-term disease prevention

Parents should follow the recommended vaccination schedule to ensure optimal protection for their children.

When Should You Visit a Child Specialist?

Consult a pediatrician if your child experiences:

  • Persistent fever
  • Frequent infections
  • Poor appetite
  • Delayed growth
  • Breathing difficulties
  • Allergies
  • Digestive problems
  • Developmental concerns
  • Skin rashes
  • Vaccination requirements

Early diagnosis and treatment help prevent complications and promote healthy development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What does a child specialist treat?

A child specialist treats illnesses, infections, growth issues, developmental concerns, allergies, respiratory conditions, digestive disorders, and provides preventive healthcare for children.

How often should my child visit a pediatrician?

Regular check-ups are recommended during infancy and childhood to monitor growth, vaccinations, and overall health.

Are vaccinations important for children?

Yes. Vaccinations help protect children from serious infectious diseases and support long-term health.

When should I take my child to a doctor for fever?

Consult a pediatrician if fever persists, becomes severe, or is accompanied by breathing difficulty, dehydration, or unusual symptoms.

Does Moraya Hospital provide vaccination services?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers vaccination and immunization services according to recommended pediatric schedules.

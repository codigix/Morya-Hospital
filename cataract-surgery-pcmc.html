What is a Cataract?

A cataract is the clouding of the natural lens of the eye, leading to blurred vision, glare sensitivity, difficulty reading, and reduced visual clarity. Cataracts commonly develop with age but may also occur due to injury, diabetes, certain medications, or other eye conditions.
Early diagnosis and timely treatment can help prevent significant vision impairment.

Symptoms of Cataracts

  • Blurred or cloudy vision
  • Difficulty seeing at night
  • Increased sensitivity to light
  • Glare from headlights
  • Frequent changes in eyeglass prescription
  • Faded or yellowed colours
  • Double vision in one eye
  • Difficulty reading or driving

Who May Need Cataract Surgery?

Cataract surgery may be recommended for individuals who experience:

  • Significant vision impairment
  • Difficulty performing daily activities
  • Problems driving at night
  • Reading difficulties
  • Reduced quality of life due to poor vision

An eye specialist can determine the right time for surgery based on symptoms and examination findings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What causes cataracts?

Cataracts commonly develop due to ageing but may also result from diabetes, eye injuries, certain medications, or genetic factors.

Is cataract surgery safe?

Cataract surgery is one of the most commonly performed eye procedures and is generally considered safe when performed by qualified eye specialists.

How long does cataract surgery take?

The procedure is usually completed within a short period, though individual treatment plans may vary.

When should I consider cataract surgery?

Surgery may be recommended when cataracts begin affecting your daily activities, vision, and overall quality of life.

Does Moraya Hospital provide cataract evaluation and surgery?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers cataract screening, surgical consultation, cataract surgery, and post-operative eye care services.

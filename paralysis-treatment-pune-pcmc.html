Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides comprehensive Paralysis Treatment in Pune and PCMC with expert neurological care, rehabilitation, physiotherapy, and recovery support. Our multidisciplinary team focuses on improving mobility, muscle strength, independence, and quality of life for patients affected by paralysis and neurological disorders.
We serve patients across Pune, PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and surrounding areas with advanced diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation programs designed to maximize recovery and functional improvement.

What is Paralysis?

JParalysis is the loss of muscle function in one or more parts of the body due to damage to the nervous system. It may affect the face, arms, legs, or one side of the body and can occur suddenly or gradually depending on the underlying cause.
Early diagnosis and rehabilitation play a crucial role in improving recovery outcomes.

Causes of Paralysis

Our specialists diagnose and manage paralysis resulting from various neurological conditions, including:

  • Stroke
  • Brain injury
  • Spinal cord injury
  • Neurological disorders
  • Nerve damage
  • Brain infections
  • Tumors affecting the nervous system
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Traumatic injuries

Symptoms That Need Immediate Medical Attention

Consult a specialist immediately if you experience:

  • Sudden weakness on one side of the body
  • Loss of movement in arms or legs
  • Facial drooping
  • Difficulty speaking
  • Loss of balance
  • Sudden numbness
  • Difficulty walking
  • Reduced coordination

Early medical intervention can significantly improve recovery outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

Can paralysis improve with treatment?

Recovery depends on the cause, severity, and timing of treatment. Early rehabilitation often improves functional outcomes.

Is physiotherapy important after paralysis?

Yes. Physiotherapy plays a vital role in improving movement, muscle strength, coordination, and overall recovery.

What is the most common cause of paralysis?

Stroke is one of the most common causes of paralysis, especially in adults.

How soon should rehabilitation begin?

Rehabilitation should begin as early as medically appropriate to maximize recovery potential.

Does Moraya Hospital provide paralysis rehabilitation?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers neurological evaluation, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and long-term recovery support for paralysis patients.

Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers advanced Cochlear Implant evaluation, counselling, and treatment support for children and adults with severe to profound hearing loss. Our experienced ENT specialists and audiology team provide comprehensive hearing assessments, implant candidacy evaluation, pre-operative counselling, and post-implant rehabilitation to help patients regain hearing and improve communication.
We serve patients across Pune, PCMC, and Chinchwad with personalised hearing care using modern diagnostic technology and patient-focused treatment approaches.

Our Cochlear Implant Services

01

Cochlear Implant Evaluation

Our specialists conduct detailed assessments to determine whether a patient is suitable for cochlear implant surgery.

Services Include::

  • Hearing assessment
  • Audiometry testing
  • Speech perception evaluation
  • ENT examination
  • Hearing aid benefit assessment
  • Cochlear implant candidacy evaluation

02

Paediatric Cochlear Implant Support

Early intervention is essential for children with severe hearing loss. Our team provides comprehensive evaluation and guidance for children who may benefit from cochlear implantation.

Services Include:

  • Newborn hearing screening follow-up
  • Childhood hearing loss assessment
  • Speech and language development evaluation
  • Parent counselling
  • Rehabilitation planning
  • Nose bleeding

03

Adult Cochlear Implant Assessment

Adults experiencing severe hearing loss can benefit from cochlear implant technology when hearing aids no longer provide sufficient benefit.

Conditions Include:

  • Severe hearing loss
  • Profound hearing loss
  • Progressive hearing loss
  • Hearing loss after infection
  • Hearing loss affecting communication and quality of life

04

Audiology & Hearing Rehabilitation

Successful cochlear implantation requires ongoing audiology support and rehabilitation.

Services Include:

  • Hearing tests
  • Audiometry
  • Speech therapy support
  • Cochlear implant mapping guidance
  • Hearing rehabilitation counselling
  • Follow-up hearing assessments

05

ENT Consultation for Hearing Loss

Our ENT specialists evaluate patients with hearing impairment and recommend appropriate treatment options based on individual needs.

Conditions Evaluated::

  • Sensorineural hearing loss
  • Congenital hearing loss
  • Progressive hearing loss
  • Single-sided deafness
  • Severe to profound deafness

Symptoms That Need Cochlear Implant Evaluation

Consult our ENT specialist if you experience:

  • Severe hearing loss
  • Difficulty understanding speech
  • Limited benefit from hearing aids
  • Delayed speech development in children
  • Difficulty communicating in daily life
  • Profound hearing impairment
  • Hearing loss affecting education or work

Early diagnosis and intervention can significantly improve hearing outcomes and communication abilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What is a cochlear implant?

A cochlear implant is an advanced electronic device that helps individuals with severe to profound hearing loss perceive sound when hearing aids provide limited benefit.

Who is eligible for a cochlear implant?

Children and adults with severe to profound sensorineural hearing loss who receive limited benefit from hearing aids may be eligible after detailed evaluation.

Is cochlear implant surgery safe?

Cochlear implant surgery is generally considered safe when performed by experienced ENT specialists after proper assessment.

How long does cochlear implant rehabilitation take?

Rehabilitation varies from patient to patient and may involve ongoing audiology sessions, speech therapy, and hearing training for optimal outcomes.

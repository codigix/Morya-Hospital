Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Orthopedic Hospital in PCMC, providing comprehensive diagnosis, treatment, and surgical care for bone, joint, muscle, ligament, and spine-related conditions. Our experienced orthopaedic specialists offer personalised treatment plans using advanced medical technology to help patients regain mobility, reduce pain, and improve quality of life.
Whether you require treatment for fractures, arthritis, sports injuries, spine disorders, or joint replacement surgery, our dedicated orthopaedic team delivers expert care for patients across PCMC, Chinchwad, Pune, and nearby areas.

Symptoms That Need Orthopaedic Consultation

Consult an orthopaedic specialist if you experience:

  • Persistent joint pain
  • Knee pain while walking
  • Back or neck pain
  • Difficulty moving joints
  • Sports-related injuries
  • Swelling around joints
  • Recurrent fractures
  • Joint stiffness
  • Difficulty standing or walking
  • Spine-related discomfort

Early diagnosis and treatment can prevent complications and improve long-term outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

When should I consult an orthopaedic doctor?

You should consult an orthopaedic specialist if you have persistent joint pain, back pain, fractures, sports injuries, or difficulty performing daily activities due to bone or muscle problems.

Do you provide knee replacement surgery?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers advanced knee replacement procedures performed by experienced orthopaedic surgeons.

Can back pain be treated without surgery?

Many cases of back pain can be managed with medication, physiotherapy, lifestyle modifications, and non-surgical treatments. Surgery is recommended only when necessary.

What conditions does a spine specialist treat?

A spine specialist treats slip disc, sciatica, cervical spondylosis, spinal stenosis, chronic back pain, and other spine-related disorders.

Do you treat sports injuries?

Yes, we provide comprehensive diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and recovery support for various sports-related injuries.

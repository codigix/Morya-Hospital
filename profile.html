About Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital

  • Speciality: Multispeciality Hospital
  • Established : 1982
  • Timings: 24 Hrs.
  • Under Trust Ashwin Medical Foundation
  • Location Chinchwad Gaon, Pune
  • Call : 020-67306500

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is an institution under the aegis of Ashwin Medical Foundation, which is a Public Charitable Trust. By the grace of the presiding deity ‘Moraya Chintamani’, the Hospital was inaugurated in August 1982 in the premises of the famous ‘Mangalmurtiwada’ in Chinchwadgaon, Pune 411 033.

With the assistance of four socially conscious doctors, in a short time, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital took shape from what was previously only an Out Patient Department. Today the very same Hospital is a renowned name that has gained the trust of just about everybody, not only from Chinchwad but also from nearby suburbans.

Despite wishing to introduce new facilities and services the existing premises of the hospital was inadequate to provide them. In order to build a spacious hospital, the Chinchwad Devasthan Trust has made available a plot of about 14000 sq. ft. near Chaphekar Chowk on a 99 years’ lease. A two-storied hospital building having bed-strength of 50 has been erected on this land, which offers various medical and surgical facilities.

"Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is an institution under the aegis of Ashwin Medical Foundation"

Full flagged 8 beded Intensive Care Unit boasts of a spacious OPD, Special Rooms, State of art Operation Theatre, Laboratory, Radiology Department having the facility of X-Ray, Sonography‚ Colour Dopller‚ Mammography‚ 2D Echo‚ Stress Test‚ PFT‚ Yog Theraphy, Physio Therapy‚ Puva Therapy & Ambulance. Along with conventional Allopathy, Ayurvedic‚ Panchkarma and Homeopathic Soforology treatments are also available here. The OPD, which is open from 8.00 am to 10.30 pm has 15 departments with 3 specialists assigned to each department. In addition to this there is a 24 hrs Casualty ward, which is supervised at all times by specially appointed doctors.We also have Government authorized Centre for M.T.P. & Family Planning Operations. These operations are done at concessional rates for economically backward patients.

From June 2007, we have started training course of patients Assistant of Y.C.M.O. University, Nasik. From 24th January 2010 we have started “Clinical Trail Centre” as well as “Soforology & Holistic Healing Centre.”

Proceedings of Morya Hospital

1982

Shri. Mangalmurthy Ward, Osarivar Baha Patient Department.

1983

Inpatient department in 2 rooms in Jalkya Wada near temple.

1987

Establishment of Ashwin Medical Foundation.

1988-1994

Out Patient Department in Vetalnagar Sevavasti.

1989

New building in the open space in Jalkya Wada.

1990-2010

National Leprosy Eradication Project successfully implemented.

2000

Listening / Speaking training for congenitally deaf children.

2003

Shifted to 3 storied building in Power House Chowk.

2005

Intensive Care Unit (ICU).

2007

Mammography facility in collaboration with Rotary Club association.

2006-2007

Health Camps, Blood Donation Camps, Workshops & Awareness Programs.

2008

Separate operation theater for eye operations.

2012

Dialysis Center started in collaboration with Lions Club Bhosari.

2014

Clinical Trials and Research Center started.

2015

Various check-up packages by setting up wellness center.

2016

Outpatient Department renewal by donation of Smt. Vrindatai Khurjekar.

Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers comprehensive nephrology services for the diagnosis, treatment, and management of kidney-related diseases. Our experienced nephrologists and renal care team provide personalised treatment using advanced medical technology and patient-focused care. We serve patients across Chinchwad, PCMC, and Pune with reliable kidney care, dialysis support, and long-term renal disease management.

Our Nephrology Services

01

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management

We provide complete evaluation and long-term management for patients with chronic kidney disease to help slow disease progression and improve kidney health.

Services Include:

  • Kidney function monitoring
  • Blood pressure management
  • Diabetes-related kidney care
  • Lifestyle and dietary guidance
  • Regular renal health assessments

02

Dialysis Services

Our hospital offers safe and advanced dialysis services under expert medical supervision for patients with kidney failure and severe renal disorders.

Dialysis Care Includes:

  • Hemodialysis
  • Emergency dialysis
  • Long-term dialysis management
  • Renal monitoring and support

03

Kidney Stone Treatment

We diagnose and treat kidney stones using advanced diagnostic facilities and personalised treatment plans to relieve pain and prevent recurrence.

Conditions Treated:

  • Kidney stones
  • Recurrent stone formation
  • Urinary blockage due to stones
  • Stone-related infections

04

Urinary Tract Infection (UTI) Treatment

Our nephrology specialists provide effective diagnosis and treatment for urinary tract infections and related kidney complications.

Common Symptoms:

  • Burning urination
  • Frequent urination
  • Lower abdominal pain
  • Fever and discomfort
  • Blood in urine

05

Hypertension & Kidney Care

High blood pressure can seriously affect kidney function. Our specialists help manage hypertension-related kidney damage with proper medical care and regular monitoring.

06

Acute Kidney Injury Treatment

We provide emergency nephrology care for sudden kidney failure caused by infections, dehydration, medication reactions, or other serious conditions.

07

Diabetic Kidney Disease Management

Patients with diabetes are at higher risk of kidney complications. We offer specialised renal care to manage diabetic nephropathy and protect long-term kidney health.

Symptoms That Need Nephrology Consultation

Consult a nephrologist if you experience:

  • Swelling in legs, feet, or face
  • Blood in urine
  • Severe back or side pain
  • Frequent urinary infections
  • High blood pressure
  • Reduced urine output
  • Persistent fatigue
  • Kidney stone pain

Early diagnosis can help prevent severe kidney complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What does a nephrologist treat?

A nephrologist treats kidney diseases, kidney stones, urinary infections, dialysis needs, hypertension-related kidney issues, and chronic kidney disorders.

Do you provide dialysis services in Chinchwad?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides advanced dialysis and renal care services.

What are the early signs of kidney disease?

Common signs include swelling, urinary problems, fatigue, blood in urine, high blood pressure, and back pain.

Can kidney disease be managed without surgery?

Many kidney diseases can be effectively managed with medications, dialysis, lifestyle changes, and regular nephrology care.

Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides comprehensive cardiac care through experienced heart specialists dedicated to the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of heart-related conditions. Our cardiology department offers advanced heart health evaluations, diagnostic services, and personalised treatment plans to help patients maintain optimal cardiovascular health.
We serve patients across PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and Pune with expert cardiac care focused on early diagnosis, effective treatment, and long-term heart wellness.

Our Cardiology Services

01

Comprehensive Heart Check-Up

Regular heart screenings can help identify cardiovascular risks before they become serious health problems.

Services Include:

  • Cardiology consultation
  • Blood pressure assessment
  • Cholesterol evaluation
  • Heart disease risk assessment
  • Preventive heart screening
  • Lifestyle counselling

02

ECG & Advanced Cardiac Diagnostics

Our hospital offers modern diagnostic services to accurately evaluate heart conditions and monitor cardiac health.

Diagnostic Services:

  • Electrocardiogram (ECG)
  • 2D Echocardiography (2D Echo)
  • Holter Monitoring
  • Stress Test (TMT)
  • Blood pressure monitoring
  • Cardiac risk evaluation

03

Chest Pain Evaluation

Chest discomfort should never be ignored. Our heart specialists evaluate symptoms to identify potential heart-related conditions and recommend appropriate treatment.

Conditions Evaluated:

  • Chest pain
  • Angina
  • Coronary artery disease
  • Heart attack risk
  • Cardiac-related discomfort

04

High Blood Pressure Treatment

Uncontrolled blood pressure can increase the risk of serious heart disease and stroke.

Conditions Managed:

  • Hypertension
  • Resistant hypertension
  • Heart disease risk factors
  • Lifestyle-related cardiovascular disorders

05

Heart Rhythm Disorder Treatment

We diagnose and manage abnormal heart rhythms that may affect heart function and overall health.

Conditions Treated:

  • Palpitations
  • Arrhythmias
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Rapid heart rate
  • Slow heart rate

06

Heart Failure Management

Our cardiology team provides ongoing care and monitoring for patients with heart failure and chronic cardiac conditions.

Services Include:

  • Cardiac function assessment
  • Medication management
  • Lifestyle modification guidance
  • Long-term heart monitoring

Preventive Cardiology Care

Prevention is one of the most effective ways to protect heart health.
Preventive Services Include:

  • Heart disease screening
  • Cholesterol management
  • Diabetes-related cardiac evaluation
  • Weight management counselling
  • Smoking cessation support
  • Healthy lifestyle guidance

Symptoms That Need a Heart Specialist Consultation

Consult a heart specialist if you experience:

  • Chest pain or chest discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • High blood pressure
  • Palpitations or irregular heartbeat
  • Dizziness or fainting
  • Excessive fatigue
  • Swelling in legs or feet
  • Family history of heart disease
  • Uncontrolled diabetes affecting heart health

Early diagnosis and treatment can help prevent serious cardiovascular complications and improve long-term health outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

When should I consult a heart specialist?

You should consult a heart specialist if you have chest pain, high blood pressure, shortness of breath, palpitations, dizziness, or a family history of heart disease.

What tests does a heart specialist perform?

Common tests include ECG, 2D Echo, Holter monitoring, stress tests, blood pressure evaluation, and comprehensive cardiac assessments.

Can high blood pressure damage the heart?

Yes, uncontrolled hypertension increases the risk of heart attack, heart failure, stroke, and other cardiovascular complications.

Do you provide preventive heart check-ups?

YYes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers preventive heart screenings, cardiac risk assessments, and comprehensive heart health evaluations.

How can I reduce my risk of heart disease?

Maintaining healthy blood pressure, controlling cholesterol, exercising regularly, eating a balanced diet, avoiding tobacco, and undergoing regular heart check-ups can help reduce cardiovascular risk.

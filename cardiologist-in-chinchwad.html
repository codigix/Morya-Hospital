Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers comprehensive cardiology services for the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of heart-related conditions. Our experienced cardiologists provide personalised heart care using advanced diagnostic technology and evidence-based treatment approaches.
We serve patients across Chinchwad, PCMC, and Pune with expert cardiac care focused on early detection, accurate diagnosis, and long-term heart health management.

  • Cardiac risk profiling

Preventive Cardiology Services

Prevention plays a crucial role in maintaining heart health.
Preventive Care Includes:

  • Heart disease risk assessment
  • Diabetes-related cardiac evaluation
  • Cholesterol management guidance
  • Lifestyle modification counselling
  • Weight management support
  • Smoking cessation guidance

Symptoms That Need Cardiology Consultation

Consult a cardiologist if you experience:

  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • High blood pressure
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Palpitations
  • Dizziness or fainting
  • Excessive fatigue
  • Swelling in legs or feet
  • Family history of heart disease

Early diagnosis and treatment can help prevent serious heart complications and improve long-term cardiovascular health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

When should I consult a cardiologist?

You should consult a cardiologist if you experience chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, high blood pressure, dizziness, or have a family history of heart disease.

What tests are performed by a cardiologist?

Common cardiac tests include ECG, 2D Echo, Holter monitoring, stress tests, blood pressure monitoring, and heart health evaluations.

Can high blood pressure affect the heart?

Yes, uncontrolled high blood pressure increases the risk of heart disease, heart failure, stroke, and other cardiovascular complications.

Do you provide preventive heart check-ups?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides preventive cardiology evaluations and heart health screening services.

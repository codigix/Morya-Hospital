Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers advanced urology services for the diagnosis and treatment of urinary tract and male reproductive system disorders. Our experienced urologists provide personalised treatment using modern diagnostic technology and patient-focused medical care.
We deliver comprehensive urology care for patients across Chinchwad, PCMC, and Pune with a focus on accurate diagnosis, effective treatment, and faster recovery.

Our Urology Services

01

Kidney Stone Treatment

We provide advanced diagnosis and treatment for kidney stones and urinary tract stones to help relieve pain and prevent complications.

Conditions Treated:

  • Kidney stones
  • Ureter stones
  • Bladder stones
  • Recurrent stone formation
  • Stone-related urinary blockage

02

Urinary Tract Infection (UTI) Treatment

Our urology specialists diagnose and treat urinary tract infections affecting the kidneys, bladder, and urinary system.

Common Symptoms:

  • Burning sensation during urination
  • Frequent urination
  • Blood in urine
  • Lower abdominal pain
  • Fever and discomfort

03

Prostate Disorder Treatment

We offer specialised care for prostate-related conditions in men, especially in older adults.

Prostate Conditions Include:

  • Enlarged prostate (BPH)
  • Prostate infections
  • Difficulty in urination
  • Urinary retention
  • Prostate health evaluation

04

Bladder Disorder Management

Our urologists provide treatment for bladder disorders that affect urinary control and comfort.

Bladder Problems Treated:

  • Overactive bladder
  • Urinary incontinence
  • Bladder infections
  • Painful urination
  • Urinary retention

05

Male Urology Care

We provide comprehensive care for male urinary and reproductive health conditions.

Services Include:

  • Male urinary problems
  • Reproductive health evaluation
  • Urological infections
  • Sexual health-related urology consultation

06

Minimally Invasive Urology Procedures

Our hospital offers minimally invasive urology procedures for faster recovery, reduced discomfort, and improved patient outcomes.

Symptoms That Need Urology Consultation

Consult a urologist if you experience:

  • Difficulty urinating
  • Blood in urine
  • Severe back or side pain
  • Frequent urination
  • Burning urination
  • Urinary leakage
  • Recurrent urinary infections

Early diagnosis and treatment can help prevent serious complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What does a urologist treat?

A urologist treats kidney stones, urinary tract infections, prostate disorders, bladder conditions, and male reproductive health problems.

Do you provide kidney stone treatment in Chinchwad?

Yes, Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides advanced kidney stone and urology treatment services.

When should I consult a urologist?

You should consult a urologist if you have urinary problems, blood in urine, kidney stone pain, prostate issues, or recurrent infections.

Are urinary tract infections serious?

If left untreated, urinary infections can affect kidney health and may lead to complications.

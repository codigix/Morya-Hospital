Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital offers comprehensive Sports Injury Treatment in Pune, providing expert diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and recovery for athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and individuals with sports-related injuries. Our experienced orthopaedic specialists and physiotherapists use evidence-based treatment approaches to help patients recover safely, regain mobility, and return to their daily activities or sports as early as possible. Most sports injuries can be successfully managed with early evaluation, physiotherapy, and rehabilitation, while severe ligament, tendon, or bone injuries may require surgical intervention.
We serve patients across Pune, PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and nearby areas with advanced orthopaedic care, physiotherapy, diagnostic imaging, and post-injury rehabilitation.

Common Sports Injuries We Treat

Our specialists diagnose and manage a wide range of sports-related injuries, including:

  • Ligament injuries (ACL, PCL, MCL)
  • Meniscus injuries
  • Ankle sprains
  • Muscle strains
  • Tendon injuries
  • Rotator cuff injuries
  • Shoulder dislocation
  • Tennis elbow
  • Golfer's elbow
  • Achilles tendon injuries
  • Knee injuries
  • Stress fractures
  • Hamstring injuries
  • Shin splints
  • Sports-related fractures

Non-Surgical Sports Injury Treatment

Many sports injuries can be effectively treated without surgery.

Treatment Options Include:

  • Activity modification

Sports Rehabilitation

Our rehabilitation programs are designed to restore strength, flexibility, balance, and performance.

Rehabilitation Includes:

  • Muscle strengthening
  • Joint mobility exercises
  • Balance training
  • Flexibility exercises
  • Functional rehabilitation
  • Return-to-sport guidance

04

Arthroscopic & Surgical Consultation

For severe ligament tears, tendon injuries, cartilage damage, or fractures, our orthopaedic surgeons provide expert surgical evaluation and advanced treatment when required.

Symptoms That Need Sports Injury Evaluation

Consult an orthopaedic specialist if you experience:

  • Persistent joint pain
  • Swelling after sports activity
  • Difficulty walking
  • Reduced range of motion
  • Muscle weakness
  • Joint instability
  • Clicking or locking of joints
  • Inability to bear weight
  • Recurrent sports injuries

Early diagnosis helps reduce recovery time and lowers the risk of long-term complications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What are the most common sports injuries?

Common injuries include ankle sprains, ligament tears, muscle strains, knee injuries, shoulder injuries, tendonitis, and stress fractures.

Do all sports injuries require surgery?

No. Most sports injuries can be managed with rest, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and medications. Surgery is generally recommended only for severe ligament tears, fractures, or tendon injuries.

When should I consult a doctor after a sports injury?

You should seek medical evaluation if pain, swelling, instability, or difficulty moving persists, or if you cannot bear weight on the injured limb.

How long does recovery take?

Recovery depends on the type and severity of the injury. Minor injuries may recover within a few weeks, while ligament injuries or fractures may require several months of rehabilitation.

Does Moraya Hospital provide physiotherapy for sports injuries?

Yes. Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital provides comprehensive physiotherapy and rehabilitation programs to support safe and effective recovery.

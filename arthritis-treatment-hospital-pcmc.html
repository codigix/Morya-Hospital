Introduction

Moraya Multispeciality Charitable Hospital is a trusted Arthritis Treatment Hospital in PCMC, providing comprehensive diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation for patients suffering from arthritis and joint-related disorders. Our experienced orthopaedic specialists and physiotherapy team offer personalised treatment plans focused on reducing pain, improving joint mobility, and enhancing quality of life.
We serve patients across PCMC, Chinchwad, Pimpri, Nigdi, Ravet, Wakad, and Pune with advanced arthritis care using modern diagnostic facilities and evidence-based treatment approaches.

Types of Arthritis We Treat

Osteoarthritis

The most common form of arthritis caused by wear and tear of joint cartilage.
Commonly Affects:

  • Knee joints
  • Hip joints
  • Shoulder joints
  • Hands and fingers
  • Spine

Rheumatoid Arthritis

An autoimmune condition that causes inflammation and damage to multiple joints.
Symptoms Include:

  • Joint pain
  • Morning stiffness
  • Swelling
  • Fatigue
  • Reduced joint function

Gout Arthritis

A form of arthritis caused by uric acid crystal deposits in joints.
Common Symptoms:

  • Sudden joint pain
  • Swelling
  • Redness
  • Tenderness in affected joints

Age-Related Joint Disorders

Joint degeneration due to ageing can lead to pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility.

What is Arthritis?

Arthritis is a condition that causes inflammation, pain, stiffness, and reduced movement in the joints. It can affect people of all ages and commonly involves the knees, hips, shoulders, hands, and spine.
Early diagnosis and treatment can help slow disease progression and improve daily functioning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick information to help patients understand our services and facilities.

What is the most common type of arthritis?

Osteoarthritis is the most common type of arthritis and typically affects the knees, hips, hands, and spine.

Can arthritis be treated without surgery?

Yes. Many patients benefit from medications, physiotherapy, exercise programs, weight management, and lifestyle modifications.

When should I consult a doctor for arthritis?

You should consult an orthopaedic specialist if you experience persistent joint pain, stiffness, swelling, or difficulty performing daily activities.

Is physiotherapy effective for arthritis?

Yes. Physiotherapy helps improve mobility, strengthen muscles, reduce stiffness, and support long-term joint function.

Do all arthritis patients need joint replacement surgery?

No. Joint replacement surgery is usually recommended only when arthritis becomes severe and conservative treatments are no longer effective.
